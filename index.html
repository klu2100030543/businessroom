05 / The person behind the vision
Meet the Founder

HEMANTH.G

Founder · THEBUSINESSROOM

HEMANTH.G brings a cross-industry perspective to storytelling, shaped by hands-on experience in technology operations, commerce, marketing, branding, business development, and communications.

Alongside his undergraduate studies, he completed internships across multiple business disciplines. After graduating, he worked in frontline YouTube operations serving a Google client, gaining experience in platform-focused processes and professional delivery.

He later pursued studies in International Strategic Marketing Management at the University of Greenwich in London, leaving the programme before completing the degree while obtaining certification. His time in London also included international-standard hospitality experience as a chef and mixologist, alongside work spanning events, business operations, and content strategy.

Drawing on these experiences, he independently founded THEBUSINESSROOM from the ground up — a strategic content production and social media management company helping local and international businesses showcase their work, their identity, and the people behind them.

Different industries. One perspective: every business has a story worth telling.
